# Cozombie privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Cozombie is a single-player game for iPhone, iPad and Android, made by
Gergely Várhelyi-Tóth. It does not
collect, store or share any personal information about you, and this
applies to everybody who plays it, children included.

- **No account.** You never sign in, and the game never asks for your name,
  email address or anything else about you.
- **No ads, no analytics, no tracking.** The game contains no advertising,
  no analytics and no tracking code, its own or anybody else's, and it does
  not use your phone's advertising identifier.
- **Nothing is sent anywhere by the game.** Everything the game needs (its
  pictures, sounds and words) is inside the app. Its own code makes no
  network connections at all.
- **Nothing to buy.** Every shelter is open to everybody, so the game does
  not ask the App Store or Google Play anything. If a purchase is ever added,
  this page will say what that involves before the version with it comes
  out.
- **Your progress stays on your device.** Your save and your settings are
  kept in the app's own storage on your phone or tablet, and nowhere else.
  Deleting the app deletes them. They are not synced between devices, but
  your phone's own backup (iCloud or a computer backup on iPhone and iPad,
  the Google backup on Android) can include them, so restoring a backup to
  a new phone brings them back.
- **Notifications are made on your phone.** If you allow them, the game sets
  itself local reminders on your phone (a job is finished, the stores are
  full). Nothing is sent to or from a server to do that, and you can switch
  them off in the game's menu or in your phone's settings.

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to ask for, correct or
delete.

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the change ships.
