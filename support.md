# Cozombie support

Cozombie is a cozy idle game about restoring shelters after the end of the
world, for iPhone, iPad and Android.

## Common questions

**How do I play?** Tap the shelter to gather by hand, then build the jobs in
the Restore tab, room by room: Next up, at the top of it, is the next job
you can start. A tap gathers what it lands on: the woodpile wood, the
workbench parts, the garden food, anywhere else scrap. The power and water
gauges at the top are the crank and the pump. The Supply tab sets up income that keeps
coming while the game is closed, for up to eight hours. The menu (the cog
at the top) has a short guide: How to play.

**Where are the settings?** In the menu: the cog at the top of the picture.
Sound and music, notifications, the screen, how builders behave, and
starting again are all there.

**Why did the lights go out?** The batteries ran flat. Lamps, turrets and
floodlights all use power at night; solar panels make it by day, less when
it is cloudy. Build more panels (Restore, Outside), light candles instead
(Defend tab), or tap the power gauge at the top to turn the crank. The
Supply tab shows what tonight will take.

**Why is everybody thirsty?** There is no water. The pump gives a little
every day; rain tanks (Restore, Outside) hold far more and fill when it
rains. Tap the water gauge at the top to work the pump.

**A shelter behind me has stopped sending anything.** It is in trouble: the
fence is down, the roof leaks, somebody has a fever. The World tab says
which. Go back there and put it right (the card at the top of the Restore
tab); it sends half as much again for a couple of days after.

**Why does it say Night 22 and not a date?** The game runs about four
hundred days for every real one, so it counts the nights you were there
for instead. The seasons turn once a real week, and letters and the story
are dated by them.

**Is the game free?** Yes. All twenty shelters are open to everybody, and
there are no ads and nothing to buy.

**My supply line is at level 10. Is that the end?** No. After level 10 it
cannot get any longer, but it can be improved for ever: each level adds
half as much as before and costs a quarter more than the last.

**The numbers at the top are in the way.** Drag the picture down. Or tap
the armchair in the corner (Just sit) to hide everything and watch; tap
again to come back.

**The writing is too small.** Open the menu, then Screen, and choose a
bigger text size. What people say is always drawn at a readable size,
however far you zoom in or out.

**What is anything for?** Tap the looking glass at the top, then tap whatever
you are wondering about. Every job in the Restore tab also says what it
changes, and its Tree button shows what each job opens.

**Why is nobody moving in?** People come when there is a bed free and the
shelter is cozy enough. A furnished bedroom sleeps two, a nursery two, a
lounge and an infirmary one each. You sleep on your own bedroll. The Crew
tab shows people and places to sleep, and how cozy the place has to be for
the next arrival.

**Who should do what?** Everybody has a knack, shown under their name in the
Crew tab, and does that job better than the others. Somebody who slept well
works harder the next day, and so does somebody whose favourite thing has
been built. Whoever is on the night watch is up all night. People who move
into a shelter you have left find a job by themselves.

**Why are my stores full?** Each shelter can only hold so much, about two
hours of what comes in. Spend it, build a store room, or move on: the
shelters behind send a share of what they make up the road to wherever you
are.

**What do I do at night?** Tap the dead before they tear through the
barricade. Build defences outside (Restore tab, Outside), put somebody on
watch (Crew tab) and mend the barricade by day (Defend tab). Nights are quiet
until you build your first lamp. The Defend tab says which kinds are coming
tonight and what works against each.

**Too many of them?** In the Defend tab, switch the lights to candles (about
half as many come) or off (very few come). A dark house is also where people
sleep best; lights on all night brings the most of the dead, and the most
salvage and samples from them, but a shallow sleep. Rain and storms also
bring fewer.

**Can I make the panel bigger?** Drag the bar above the tabs all the way up,
or flick it up: the panel then covers the whole screen. Drag or flick it
down again, or tap the bar, to go back.

**The small print in the tabs is too much.** Tap the × on any tip to put it
away. Menu, then How to play, has Show all the tips again.

**What does the weather do?** The Defend tab shows today's and the next two
days'. Rain fills the rain tanks, helps the garden and thins the dead, but
slows scavenging and wood. Fog hides the dead from the turrets until they
are close. On a night of snow or frost you need a stove or a fire pit and
some wood, or everybody is cold and works more slowly the next day.

**Does it carry on while it is closed?** Yes, for up to eight hours:
supplies come in until the stores are full, jobs finish and builders start
the next one. Whether builders choose their own jobs is in the menu, under
Play. When you come back, the game lists what happened.

**Notifications.** The first time you start a job that takes more than a
minute, the game asks whether it may tell you when things are done. You can
change your mind, and choose which kinds you get, in the menu under
Notifications, or in your phone's settings.

**Where has the game gone from my home screen?** On Android, an earlier
version changed the game's icon when you had been away (the treehouse with
its lights low), and on some phones that took the game off the home screen.
The game no longer does that: it puts the usual icon back once, the first
time you leave it after updating, and never changes it again. If the game is
missing from your home screen, it is still in the list of all your apps:
drag it back from there. On iPhone and iPad the icon never changed.

**The screen stays on.** While you are playing, and for five minutes after
your last touch. It does not when your phone is saving battery. To switch
it off, open the menu and choose Screen.

**Can I have it dark, or light?** The game follows your phone. To choose,
open the menu and choose Screen.

**Where is my progress saved?** On your device, in the game's own storage.
It is not synced between phones, and deleting the app deletes it. Your
phone's own backup does include it (iCloud or a computer backup on iPhone
and iPad, the Google backup on Android), so restoring that backup onto a new
phone brings your progress with it.

**How do I start again?** Open the menu, choose About and starting again,
then Start again from nothing.

**How do I turn off the sound or music?** The menu, Sound. Each has its own
volume there too.

## Contact

Something not working, a question, or an idea: open an issue at
[github.com/tgergo1/cozombie-site/issues](https://github.com/tgergo1/cozombie-site/issues)
(a free GitHub account is needed). Say which phone or tablet it is, and what
happened.

If the game ever says a save could not be used, it keeps a copy of it, set
aside on your device: get in touch before starting again, in case it can
be put right.

See also the [privacy policy](privacy.md).
